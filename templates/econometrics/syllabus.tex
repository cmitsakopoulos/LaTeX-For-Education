\section*{Course Overview \& Objectives}
ECON 401 equips students with modern econometric tools for causal identification using observational and quasi-experimental data. Key topics include potential outcomes, Two-Way Fixed Effects, Difference-in-Differences, Instrumental Variables (2SLS), and cluster-robust inference.

\section*{15-Week Curriculum Roadmap}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}
    \item \textbf{Weeks 1--3:} Potential outcomes framework, SUTVA, and selection on observables.
    \item \textbf{Weeks 4--6:} Panel data models: Pooled OLS, Random Effects, and Within-Group Fixed Effects.
    \item \textbf{Weeks 7--8:} Difference-in-Differences, parallel trends, and staggered rollouts.
    \item \textbf{Week 9:} \textbf{Midterm Examination}.
    \item \textbf{Weeks 10--12:} Instrumental variables, 2SLS, weak instrument diagnostics, and LATE.
    \item \textbf{Weeks 13--15:} Cluster-robust inference, Liang-Zeger standard errors, and bootstrap inference.
\end{itemize}
