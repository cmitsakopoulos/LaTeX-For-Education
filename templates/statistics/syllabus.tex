\section*{Course Overview \& Objectives}
This course provides an advanced introduction to mathematical probability and statistical inference. Students develop rigorous mathematical foundations in measure-theoretic probability basics, characteristic functions, Central Limit asymptotics, maximum likelihood estimation, Gaussian hypothesis testing, and contingency table association.

\section*{15-Week Curriculum Roadmap}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}
    \item \textbf{Weeks 1--3:} Probability spaces, Kolmogorov axioms, conditional probability, Bayes' rule.
    \item \textbf{Weeks 4--6:} Random variables, joint distributions, expectation, variance, covariance matrices.
    \item \textbf{Weeks 7--8:} Moment generating functions, Law of Large Numbers, Central Limit Theorem.
    \item \textbf{Week 9:} \textbf{Midterm Examination}.
    \item \textbf{Weeks 10--12:} Maximum likelihood estimation, asymptotic normality, Fisher information.
    \item \textbf{Weeks 13--15:} Categorical inference, contingency tables, $\chi^2$ tests of independence, Odds Ratios.
\end{itemize}
