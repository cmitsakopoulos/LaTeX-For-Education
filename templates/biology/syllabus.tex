\section*{Course Overview \& Objectives}
BIO 401 introduces students to mathematical and physical principles in biology. Topics include thermodynamic energy dissipation in ecosystems, Lindeman trophic transfer laws, enzyme kinetics (Michaelis-Menten, Hill cooperativity), receptor-ligand binding cascades, and non-linear population dynamics.

\section*{15-Week Curriculum Roadmap}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}
    \item \textbf{Weeks 1--3:} Bio-energetics, thermodynamics of living systems, Lindeman trophic efficiency.
    \item \textbf{Weeks 4--6:} Trophic energy pyramids, food webs, and nutrient cycling cascades.
    \item \textbf{Weeks 7--8:} Single-substrate enzyme kinetics, Briggs-Haldane QSSA, and Lineweaver-Burk plots.
    \item \textbf{Week 9:} \textbf{Midterm Examination}.
    \item \textbf{Weeks 10--12:} Allosteric regulation, Hill cooperativity, and GPCR signal transduction cascades.
    \item \textbf{Weeks 13--15:} Lotka-Volterra predator-prey dynamics, phase plane analysis, and stability.
\end{itemize}
